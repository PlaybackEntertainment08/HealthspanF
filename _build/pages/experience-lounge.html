What is measured, and what it is worth

The tier shown beside each station refers to its primary claim — the thing the test is usually sold on. A tier IV station is not useless; it is a station whose marketing has run ahead of its outcome data, and knowing that is the point of standing in front of it.

How to use it

Be a patient for twenty minutes

The lounge exists for one reason: a clinician who has had a biological-age report handed to them, with no explanation, describes that experience differently afterwards.

  1. Scan your delegate badge at the first station. Everything you do is collected under one record.
  2. Work through as many stations as you like, in any order. Grip strength takes five minutes; the biomarker panel needs a draw and returns a report by evening.
  3. Read the tier card at each station before you see your result. Then notice how much you want the number to be good.
  4. Take your results to the consultation desk. A clinician will read them with you, including the parts that mean nothing.

Your data

Results belong to you. They are not shared with any sponsor, vendor or partner, in identifiable or aggregated form.

You may download everything from your dashboard for ninety days after the summit. After that it is deleted from our systems — not archived, deleted.

Participation is optional at every station, and you may stop a test at any point without giving a reason.

Where a station is operated by an industry partner, the partner may not retain your data and may not contact you afterwards. See the privacy notice.

This is not a health screeningThe Experience Lounge is an educational demonstration for healthcare professionals. It is not a medical assessment, no clinical relationship is created, and no result should be acted on without proper investigation through your own physician. If a reading concerns you, the consultation desk will tell you what to do about it.
