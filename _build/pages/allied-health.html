Why a separate stream

The people who actually run the protocol

A physician prescribes resistance training. Someone else teaches the patient to do it, checks their form, and notices in week three that they have stopped coming.

Most longevity protocols fail at delivery rather than at prescription. The allied health stream exists because the nurses, technicians, physiotherapists, dietitians and counsellors who do the delivering have a different set of problems from the people writing the plans — and a different, usually better, view of which parts of a protocol survive contact with a real patient.

The stream runs in Hall III through the Day One afternoon. Allied health delegates may attend any session in any hall; the stream is addition, not confinement.
