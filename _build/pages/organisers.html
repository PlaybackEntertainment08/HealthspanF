IASRM

The International Association of Stemcell & Regenerative Medicine

A professional body for clinicians and scientists working in regenerative medicine, founded to hold the field to the same evidentiary standard as the rest of medicine.

IASRM exists because the gap between what regenerative medicine can demonstrate and what it is sold on had grown wide enough to harm patients. Its work is unglamorous: grading evidence, publishing position statements, running training that teaches practitioners to say “the data for that is thin”, and maintaining a register of adverse events that members are expected to report into.

The association does not endorse products, does not accept sponsorship for its position statements, and does not certify clinics.

What it does between summits

Standards

Position statements

Graded statements on interventions members are asked about — what the evidence supports, and at what tier.

Safety

Adverse-event register

A reporting route for serious events following cell, exosome and orthobiologic therapy, open to audit by the referring physician.

Education

Training & fellowships

Hands-on courses in regenerative technique, and the consultation skills to accompany them.

Publication

The Charter

From April 2027, custody of the signed Charter and its published voting record. Read the drafts.

Membership

Open to registered medical practitioners, researchers, allied health professionals and postgraduate trainees working in regenerative or longevity medicine. Members receive the position statements as they are issued, reduced summit registration, and access to the training programme.

Membership is not required to attend this summit, and attending does not confer it.
