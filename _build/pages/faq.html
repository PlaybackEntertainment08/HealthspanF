Attending

Invited healthcare professionals — physicians, surgeons, researchers, allied health professionals, postgraduate trainees and industry delegates working in longevity and regenerative medicine. Registration asks for your medical council registration number or institutional affiliation. It is not open to the general public.

New Delhi, in the Aerocity district beside Indira Gandhi International Airport. The property is confirmed in December 2026 and registered delegates are emailed the address and floor plan before it appears on this site. See venue & Delhi.

English throughout, including the panels and the Charter session.

Plenary sessions are recorded and released to registered delegates for ninety days afterwards, where the speaker has consented. Panel II — the Charter session — is deliberately not recorded, so that delegates can argue without worrying about a clip.

Accreditation is sought from the Delhi Medical Council for the main programme and for each accredited workshop separately. Points are confirmed in February 2027 and printed on your attendance certificate. Enter your registration number in your profile and scan in at the hall door.

Business dress for the sessions; the halls run cool, so bring a layer. For the Gala Dinner on 10 April, black tie is optional and Indian formal is equally welcome.

Fees, invoicing and refunds

Both days of the scientific programme, the rapid-fire hall, the Experience Lounge, the delegate kit with all three launched books, both luncheons, refreshments, the Gala Dinner on 10 April, trade exhibition access and the attendance certificate. Residential passes add two nights with breakfast. Full inclusions.

Travel, access and everything else

Apply under the e-Business visa (e-B5V) category — the e-Conference visa no longer exists. Register and pay, then submit your passport details through the dashboard; we issue an invitation letter within five working days and apply for Ministry approval on your behalf. Start by 31 October 2026 if you can. Full guidance.

If a visa is refused despite a Ministry approval letter issued in your name, your fee is refunded in full less bank charges, on production of the written refusal.
