What the faculty agree to

  1. An evidence tier on every slide that makes an empirical claim.
  2. A declaration of interests on the opening slide and in the published abstract.
  3. Fifteen minutes, kept. The chair closes the microphone at time.
  4. Questions taken from the floor, with the back of the hall called first.

Rapid-fire speakers

Thirty slots, chosen from submissions

The thirty rapid-fire speakers are not invited. They are selected blind from submitted abstracts by the scientific committee, and announced in January 2027.

That is the one route onto this stage that does not depend on who already knows you.
