The rules

Submission guidelines

Up to 300 words under four headings: Background, Methods, Results, Conclusion. The title may run to 160 characters. One table or figure may be attached and is not counted in the word limit.

An abstract whose Results section says only that an intervention “showed improvement” is returned unreviewed. Give the effect size, the n, and a measure of precision.

State the highest tier your work supports — I human randomised, II human observational, III animal, IV mechanism alone. Over-claiming a tier is the single most common reason for rejection. The house rule.

Equity, advisory roles, speaking fees, grant funding and ownership of any clinic delivering the intervention discussed. The declaration is published alongside the abstract. “None” is a complete answer where it is true.

Human studies require an institutional ethics committee reference. Case reports and case series require documented patient consent for publication. Identifiable images require separate written consent.

Blind. Two reviewers per abstract, a third on disagreement. Reviewers recuse themselves from their own institution’s submissions. Scored on the strength of the evidence, the honesty of the tier claimed, clinical relevance, and whether six minutes can carry it.

You may submit before registering. But the presenting author must be a registered delegate by 28 February 2027, or the slot passes to the next abstract on the list.

Actively wanted. Round III exists partly for the case that went wrong, presented by the clinician who did it. The committee weights such submissions up, not down.

Research Excellence Award

One award per summit, judged live by a panel of three from the shortlisted papers presented on Day One. The citation is read at the Honours Ceremony that evening.

Open to any registered delegate. First author must present. No entry fee.

Writing your first abstract?The secretariat will read a draft and comment once, without prejudice to the blind review, if you send it before 15 January 2027.
