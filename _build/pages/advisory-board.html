What the board does

An advisory role, not an executive one

The board advises. It does not run the summit, select the faculty, or control the budget — those sit with the organising team and the scientific committee respectively.

01

Reads the Charter before it circulates

Each draft statement is sent to the board in January 2027. Their comments are incorporated, or recorded as dissent, before delegates receive the text.

02

Advises on the programme

Where a session reads as parochial, or a topic has a stronger evidence base abroad than the draft programme reflects, the board says so.

03

Supplies the independent honours panel

Several Longevity 40 panel members are drawn from the board precisely because they sit outside the Indian professional network the nominations come from.

04

Carries the Charter home

After April 2027 the board offers the adopted Charter to societies in its own regions for endorsement.

Terms of appointment

  1. Appointed for the summit cycle, not for life.
  2. Interests declared on appointment and published alongside the name.
  3. No seat on the organising team or the scientific committee at the same time.
  4. Unpaid. Travel to the summit is covered; there is no honorarium.
