09:00 – 13:00 · 40 places

Regenerative Skills & Ultrasound-Guided Injection

Preparation and handling of PRP and bone-marrow aspirate concentrate, then ultrasound-guided injection on phantoms and live models. Knee, shoulder, hip and tendon approaches. Faculty-to-delegate ratio of one to eight.

Hands-on₹ 9,000 / USD 110CME credit

09:00 – 12:30 · 60 places

The Longevity Consultation

How to run a first longevity consultation in forty minutes: what to ask, what to order, what to decline to order, and how to say “the evidence for that is tier four” without losing the patient. Role-played with trained simulated patients.

Simulation₹ 7,500 / USD 90CME credit

14:00 – 17:30 · 50 places

Point-of-Care Assessment in a Busy OPD

Grip dynamometry, gait speed, sit-to-stand, body composition and a sub-maximal VO₂ estimate — all of it done in under ten minutes per patient, with the equipment a clinic can actually afford. You leave with a one-page protocol.

Hands-on₹ 6,500 / USD 80CME credit

14:00 – 17:00 · 80 places

Trainee Masterclass: Reading a Longevity Paper

Four published papers, one per hour, read live with the room — including one that is widely cited and should not be. How to find the effect size, the comparator, the funding and the thing the abstract did not say.

For PG traineesFree with trainee pass

By invitation · 17:30 – 19:00

Faculty & Chairs Briefing

All session chairs, lecturers and rapid-fire speakers. The house rule, the timing discipline, how the floor is managed, and the procedure for Panel II. Followed by the faculty dinner.

CME accreditation

Accreditation is sought from the Delhi Medical Council for the main programme and for each accredited workshop separately.

Credit points are confirmed in February 2027 and printed on your attendance certificate, which is issued from your dashboard within seven days of the summit closing.

To receive credit you must enter your medical council registration number in your profile and scan in at the hall door.
