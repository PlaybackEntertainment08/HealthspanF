Convening body

IASRM

International Association of Stemcell & Regenerative Medicine

Convenes the summit, appoints the scientific committee and the Longevity 40 panel, and takes custody of the signed Charter afterwards. Based in New Delhi.

In collaboration with

Anti-Ageing Foundation (India)

The clinical-practice partner

Brings the practitioners who see these patients every week — the physicians fielding questions about supplements, hormone therapy and clinics abroad. The Foundation shapes the sessions on consultation practice, and co-chairs Panel III on cost and access.

Its members also staff the consultation desk in the Experience Lounge, so that a delegate who takes a test can have the result read with them by someone who does it for a living.

In collaboration with

Entrepreneur Forum of India

The industry and access partner

Convenes the founders, manufacturers and investors building in this field, and co-chairs Panel III · Who Can Afford This? — the session on cost, access and the ethics of private longevity practice.

The Forum's presence is deliberate. A summit that argues about access without anyone in the room who sets prices is a summit having a comfortable conversation with itself.

Title sponsor

AYUVA

Funds the summit. Does not shape it.

Named in the event lockup and credited throughout. Like every partner, AYUVA has no say in the faculty, the panel motions, the Charter text or the honours — and that separation is published rather than merely promised.

What sponsorship does and does not buy

Title sponsor

For societies

Affiliate, or endorse the Charter

Two separate things, and a society may do either without the other.

Affiliation

Co-convene a session

A society with a relevant membership may propose and chair a session, or run a parallel stream. Affiliation carries no fee in either direction and no exhibition rights.

Endorsement

Adopt the Charter after Delhi

From May 2027 the adopted Charter is offered to member societies for endorsement, with the voting record attached so that anyone endorsing it can see what was argued and what carried. Endorsing bodies are listed here.
