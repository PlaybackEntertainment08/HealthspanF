Step by step

The visa process

  1. Register and pay. Visa support is issued to confirmed delegates only. Register early — the Ministry route takes months, not weeks.
  2. Send us your details. Through the visa panel of your dashboard: the eleven items listed opposite, a scan of your passport bio page and a short CV.
  3. Invitation letter. Issued by the secretariat within five working days of a complete submission.
  4. Ministry approval. The organisers apply to the Ministry of External Affairs on your behalf. The MEA approval letter is emailed to you and forwarded by the MEA to the Indian Embassy or High Commission covering your location.
  5. Apply for the e-B5V. Only once you hold every document. Apply at the official portal, indianvisaonline.gov.in — and nowhere else.

What we provide

Document one

Invitation letter from the conference organisers, naming you, your affiliation and the dates of the summit.

Document two

Ministry approvals from the Ministry of External Affairs and the Ministry of Home Affairs, with the approval reference quoted on your application.

What we need from you

  • Name exactly as in your passport
  • Nationality
  • Date of birth
  • Place of birth
  • Passport number
  • Passport expiry date
  • Passport issuing authority / place of issue
  • Current affiliation and designation
  • Address as in your passport, or for correspondence
  • Scanned copy of the passport bio page
  • A short CV
Submit these in your dashboard
Passport validity is mandatory Your passport must be valid for at least six months beyond the date of travel or application, and must have enough blank pages for immigration stamping. A passport short of either is refused at the application stage, not at the airport.
Apply only on the government portalIndian e-visas are issued at indianvisaonline.gov.in. Look-alike sites charge a fee for a form you can complete free. The secretariat never charges for visa support.

If your visa is refused

If a visa is refused despite a Ministry approval letter issued in your name, your registration fee is refunded in full less bank charges, on production of the written refusal. See the refund policy.

Arriving in Delhi

Practical notes

Weather in April

Hot and dry. Daytime 35–40 °C, evenings pleasant at 22–25 °C. Halls are air-conditioned to around 23 °C — bring a layer for indoors, and drink more water than you think you need.

Airport & transfer

Indira Gandhi International (DEL), Terminal 3 for most international arrivals. Aerocity is about ten minutes by road. Residential packages include an arrival transfer; otherwise use the prepaid taxi counter or a ride-hailing app.

Money & connectivity

Cards and UPI are accepted almost everywhere. Carry a small amount of cash for taxis and markets. Airport SIM counters are open 24 hours; bring your passport and a photograph.

Health

No vaccinations are required for entry from most countries; check your own national guidance. Drink bottled or filtered water. Travel insurance covering medical repatriation is strongly advised.

Dress

Business dress for the scientific sessions. For the Gala Dinner, black tie is optional and Indian formal is equally welcome.

Staying longer

Agra is a three-hour drive or a two-hour train. Jaipur is five hours by road. Day tours and the Agra extension are bookable through the secretariat — see Delhi tours.
