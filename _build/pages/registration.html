Cancellation & refunds

If your plans change

Written notice receivedRefund
On or before 30 June 202650% of the fee, excluding GST
1 July 2026 – 31 January 202725% of the fee, excluding GST
After 31 January 2027No refund
  • All refunds are processed after the summit has closed.
  • A registration may be transferred to a colleague at no charge until 31 March 2027. Write to the secretariat with both names.
  • If a visa is refused despite a Ministry approval letter issued in your name, the fee is refunded in full less bank charges, on production of the refusal.
  • GST, once remitted, cannot be refunded by the organisers.

Payment methods

  • Credit and debit card — Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, Amex
  • UPI, net banking and wallets (Indian delegates)
  • NEFT / RTGS / IMPS against a proforma invoice
  • SWIFT wire transfer for international delegates

A GST invoice is issued within 48 hours of payment and is available from your delegate dashboard. Add your GSTIN during registration if you need input credit.

We will never ask for payment by direct messageThe secretariat does not contact delegates on WhatsApp or social media asking for card details, OTPs or transfers to a personal account. Pay only through this site or an invoice sent from an @iasrmglobal.org address.

Group bookings of ten or more

Institutions sending ten or more delegates are invoiced directly, with a single GST invoice and one named coordinator. Write to the secretariat for a quotation.
