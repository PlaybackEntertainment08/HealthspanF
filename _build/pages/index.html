Part I · The Preamble

WE, THE RESEARCHERS, SCIENTISTS, INNOVATORS, INDUSTRIALISTS AND PRACTITIONERS OF LONGEVITY & REGENERATIVE MEDICINE,

having solemnly resolved to constitute our discipline into an EVIDENCE-LED, ETHICAL, EQUITABLE and HUMANE science, and to secure to every patient JUSTICE of honest counsel, every claim graded by its evidence; LIBERTY of informed consent where the data is thin; EQUALITY of access beyond the reach of wealth alone; and FRATERNITY, assuring the dignity of the patient and the healthspan of the nation;

in our Summit at New Delhi this eleventh day of April, 2027, do

HEREBY ADOPT, ENACT AND GIVE TO OURSELVES THIS CHARTER.

Read the full Charter

Why we gather

Most of us will spend the last decade of life unwell.

That decade, not the length of the life around it, is the problem worth solving. The global gap between lifespan and healthspan averaged 9.6 years in 2019, and it is widening.

Interest in longevity science has outpaced its evidence. This summit is built on the opposite principle.

Source: Garmany et al., JAMA Network Open, 2024 (183 WHO member states).

Who it is for

For the physician asked every week about a supplement, a test or a clinic abroad. For the researcher whose careful trial is drowned out by a louder claim. For the founder building something that should outlast its marketing. And for the patient, who is the reason for all of it.

How the Charter is made

Read. Argued. Amended. Signed.

  1. The consensus statements are read from the floor, one by one.
  2. They are argued: evidence, consent, advertising, regulation, and what a clinician owes a patient when the data is thin.
  3. Delegates vote on each; amendments carried are incorporated.
  4. Signed on Sunday afternoon by those who choose to be bound by them.
