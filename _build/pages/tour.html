Half day · 9 or 12 April

Old Delhi: Shahjahanabad on foot

The Red Fort, Jama Masjid and the lanes of Chandni Chowk, walked rather than driven, with a cycle-rickshaw stretch through Kinari Bazaar. Finishes at a Mughlai lunch.

Four hours · moderate walking · from ₹ 3,500 per person

Half day · 9 or 12 April

New Delhi: Lutyens and the Mughals

Humayun’s Tomb, the Qutub Minar complex, India Gate and a drive through the Lutyens avenues. The two UNESCO sites that bracket Delhi’s Mughal centuries.

Four hours · light walking · from ₹ 3,200 per person

Evening · 9, 10 or 11 April

The Delhi food trail

Six stops across Old Delhi and Nizamuddin, from kebabs to kulfi, with a guide who can tell you which stall has been doing one thing for sixty years. A vegetarian route runs in parallel.

Three hours · evening · from ₹ 2,800 per person

Half day · daily

Spiritual Delhi

The Lotus Temple, Akshardham and Gurudwara Bangla Sahib, including the langar kitchen that feeds several thousand people a day. Modest dress and covered heads; scarves provided.

Four hours · light walking · from ₹ 3,000 per person

Full day · 12 April

The Agra extension — the Taj Mahal at sunrise

Leaves Aerocity at 05:00 by road or on the Gatimaan Express, at the Taj for the opening of the gates, then Agra Fort and lunch before returning by early evening. The single most common reason delegates extend their stay by a day, and worth it.

Twelve hours · from ₹ 9,500 per person including entry, guide and lunch · minimum six, maximum twenty-four
