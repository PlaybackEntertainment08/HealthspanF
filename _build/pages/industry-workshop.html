The position

Industry sessions exist, and they are marked

Pretending a summit has no commercial content is worse than labelling it. So it is labelled.

Companies at Platinum tier and above may run a thirty-minute symposium in a dedicated slot. These sessions are useful — a manufacturer explaining a device's release testing, or an assay's validation data, is often the only person in the building who can answer that properly.

What makes them safe is not hiding them. It is five rules, applied without exception.

  1. Labelled everywhere. “Industry symposium, supported by [company]” appears in the printed programme, in the delegate app, on the hall screen before the session, and is read aloud by the chair.
  2. The house rule applies. Every slide making an empirical claim carries its evidence tier. Decks are audited before the hall opens, exactly as faculty decks are. The house rule.
  3. No CME credit. Industry symposia are not accredited and do not count toward the credit printed on your certificate. Attending is free; it simply earns nothing.
  4. Scheduled against nothing. They run in dedicated slots, never opposite a plenary session, so no delegate has to choose between a scientific session and a sponsored one.
  5. The floor still gets questions. At least ten of the thirty minutes belong to the audience, and the chair is a member of the scientific committee, not of the company.

What it never buys

  • A place on the scientific faculty
  • Any influence on session titles or panel motions
  • Any Longevity 40 honour or shortlist place
  • Exemption from the evidence-tier rule
  • The delegate list or any delegate's details
  • A word of the Charter

Published so that a sponsor who objects finds out now rather than in March.

Availability

Four symposium slots across the two days, offered to Platinum tier and above in order of agreement signed.

Experience Lounge stations

The other commercial surface

A limited number of assessment stations are operated by diagnostics and device partners.

Each station displays the evidence tier behind its primary claim — written by the scientific committee, not by the vendor. The partner may not retain a delegate's readings and may not contact them afterwards. Results belong to the delegate and are deleted after ninety days.
