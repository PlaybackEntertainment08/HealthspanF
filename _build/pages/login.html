How registration works

  1. Create your profile. Name, email, mobile and whether you are billing in rupees or dollars.
  2. Choose your pass. Two-day, couple, group, residential, trainee, allied health or accompanying person — plus any add-ons.
  3. Pay. Card, UPI, net banking or bank transfer. The gateway handles the card; this site never sees it.
  4. You are registered. GST invoice within 48 hours, visa letter on request, hotel confirmed, abstract submission unlocked.
Postgraduate traineesUpload a letter from your head of department or a current student identity card during registration. The reduced rate is released once it is verified, normally within two working days.
Beware of unofficial agentsThe secretariat never asks for payment through social media or to a personal account. Register only here, or through an invoice sent from an @iasrmglobal.org address.
