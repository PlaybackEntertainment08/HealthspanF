Venue announced December 2026Contracts are finalised with a five-star convention property in the Aerocity district, three kilometres from Indira Gandhi International Airport. Registered delegates are emailed the name, address and floor plan as soon as it is signed.

How the space works

Three halls, a lounge and an exhibition floor, all on one level.

Hall I

The main hall

All eleven plenary sessions, the three panels, the three book launches, the Honours Ceremony and the adoption of the Charter. Seats 900.

Hall II

Rapid-fire

Five rounds on Day Two, running in parallel with Hall I. Theatre-style, timer on the screen. Seats 300.

Hall III

Allied health & awards

The allied health and nursing stream, the Research Excellence Award papers and the Video Showcase. Seats 180.

Lounge & floor

Experience Lounge and exhibition

Eight assessment stations and the trade exhibition, open through both days. The e-poster gallery runs along the lounge wall.

Getting there

By air
Indira Gandhi International (DEL). Terminal 3 handles most international arrivals; about ten minutes by road to Aerocity.
By metro
Airport Express (Orange Line) to Aerocity station, then a short walk. New Delhi railway station is twenty minutes on the same line.
By road
Aerocity sits on NH-48 beside the airport. Allow 45–75 minutes from central or south Delhi in the morning peak.
By rail
New Delhi (NDLS) and Hazrat Nizamuddin (NZM) are the two main long-distance stations.

Accessibility

The venue is step-free throughout, with lift access to every hall, accessible toilets on the conference level and reserved seating at the front and rear of each hall. A hearing loop is installed in Halls I and II.

Tell us what you need in the access field of your profile, or write to the secretariat. Requests made before 1 March 2027 can be arranged in full; later ones we will do our best with.

Delhi in April

Hot and dry — 35–40 °C by day, 22–25 °C in the evening. Halls run at about 23 °C, so bring a layer for indoors. The light in the early morning is the best of the day; the yoga session at 07:00 is on the lawn for a reason.

Prayer & quiet room

A quiet room is set aside on the conference level for prayer, feeding, or simply sitting out a session. No booking needed.
