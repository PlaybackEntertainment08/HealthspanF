Booking your own room

If you prefer to book direct, ask for the Healthspan Standard 2027 delegate rate when you reserve. The code is issued with your registration confirmation and is valid from 8 to 13 April 2027, subject to availability.

Double occupancy & accompanying persons

Residential packages are priced single. Add an accompanying person during registration and the secretariat will quote the double-occupancy supplement directly with the hotel.

Extending your stay

Nights outside 9–11 April can usually be added at the delegate rate. Ask the secretariat before you book elsewhere — the block rate is normally better.

Housing-agency scamsNo third-party agency is authorised to arrange rooms for this summit. If you are contacted by a “housing bureau” claiming to hold your reservation, do not pay — forward the message to info@iasrmglobal.org.

Why Aerocity

Three kilometres from the aircraft door

Aerocity is a hotel district built beside Indira Gandhi International Airport. For a two-day summit with a large international faculty, it removes the single most common reason a delegate misses the opening session: Delhi traffic.
