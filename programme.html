Scientific Programme

Eleven sessions across two days. Each session: a chair and three or four lectures of fifteen minutes, then questions from the floor.

Saturday 10 April 2027 · Hall I

Measure, then treat

Measurement, metabolism, nutrition, movement and regeneration, closing with the first panel and the inaugural honours.

07:00 – 08:00
Yoga, Breathwork & Meditation
Optional. Led practice on the lawn. The programme proper begins at 08:00.
08:00 – 08:55
Registration, Welcome Coffee & Opening Address
Delegate kits, the Experience Lounge opens, and the case for a healthspan standard.
08:55 – 09:25
Book Launch: The Healthspan Standard
Unveiled from the stage, with a reading and a short conversation with the authors.
09:25 – 10:10
Session I · Foundations: The Biology and Measurement of Ageing
Hallmarks of ageing in 2027 · biological age clocks and clock drift · why grip strength and gait speed outperform most panels · what a longevity work-up should and should not contain.
Tier I–II4 lectures
10:10 – 11:10
Session II · Obesity, Metabolic Health and the Indian Phenotype
Insulin resistance at lower BMI · visceral and hepatic fat · GLP-1 receptor agonist outcome data · pharmacotherapy, muscle loss and the bariatric referral.
Tier I4 lectures
11:10 – 11:30
Break · Trade exhibition & Experience Lounge
11:30 – 12:15
Session III · Gut Health and Nutrition
The microbiome and metabolic health, soberly · protein, sarcopenia and the vegetarian patient · fasting and dietary pattern · what a stool test can and cannot tell you.
Tier II–III3 lectures
12:15 – 13:00
Session IV · Exercise, Strength and the Measured Body
Muscle as an endocrine organ · sarcopenic obesity · prescribing resistance training like a drug — dose, frequency and adherence · VO₂max as the strongest single predictor.
Tier I3 lectures
13:00 – 14:00
Luncheon
Seated. Experience Lounge remains open.
14:00 – 14:30
Book Launch: Regenerative Intelligence
14:30 – 15:30
Session V · Regenerative Medicine: The Human Evidence
Mesenchymal stromal cell trials — what has read out and what has not · exosomes and the secretome · PRP and orthobiologics in osteoarthritis · skin as an ageing organ.
Tier I–III4 lectures
15:30 – 15:50
Break
15:50 – 16:35
Session VI · Cancer Risk and Early Detection
The screening register · multi-cancer early detection panels · whole-body imaging, incidental findings, diagnostic cascades and consent.
Tier I–II3 lectures
16:35 – 17:20
Panel I · Does Longevity Science Generalise?
Motion: “Protocols developed in Western cohorts can be applied to Indian patients without modification.” Audience votes before and after; the swing is reported from the stage.
17:20 – 18:15
Interval · Change for the evening
18:15 – 21:15
The Longevity 40 Honours Ceremony & Gala Dinner
Citations read from the stage, then a seated dinner with the honourees, faculty and partners. Black tie optional; Indian formal equally welcome.

Sunday 11 April 2027 · Hall I

Argue, then sign

Sleep, brain, hormones, sexual health and technology — with five rapid-fire rounds running in Hall II, two working panels, and the Charter.

07:00 – 08:40
Yoga, Coffee & Opening of the Second Day
A shorter practice, the final window at the assessment stations, and the questions Day One left open.
08:40 – 09:05
Book Launch: The 10 PM Club
09:05 – 09:50
Session VII · Sleep, Circadian Health and Precision Longevity
The measurable cost of late nights · sleep architecture and what consumer trackers actually measure · stratification and pharmacogenomics.
Tier I–II3 lectures
09:50 – 10:35
Session VIII · Neurodegeneration and Cognitive Longevity
Detecting decline before symptoms · the anti-amyloid trials read honestly · hearing, sleep, vascular health and dementia · what to tell the worried-well patient.
Tier I3 lectures
10:35 – 10:50
Break
10:50 – 11:35
Session IX · Hormones, Peptides and Pharmacology
Testosterone therapy after TRAVERSE · menopausal hormone therapy after WHI · peptides sold ahead of data · metformin, rapamycin and the trials that are running.
Tier I–IV4 lectures
11:35 – 12:20
Session X · Sexual Health and Healthspan
ED as a cardiovascular sentinel · female sexual function through menopause · regenerative approaches and honest counselling.
Tier I–III3 lectures
12:20 – 13:10
Luncheon
13:10 – 14:05
Panel II · Where Is the Line? The Statements, Argued
The consensus statements are read from the floor, argued and voted on. A working session, not a presentation. Read the drafts.
14:05 – 14:45
Panel III · Who Can Afford This?
Cost, access and the ethics of private longevity practice, with the Entrepreneur Forum of India.
14:45 – 15:45
Session XI · Technologies in Longevity
Wearables and signal quality · CGM in the non-diabetic patient · AI in clinical practice · the digital clinic and patient privacy.
Tier II–III4 lectures
15:45 – 16:30
Adoption of the Charter & Closing Address
The amended statements are read a final time and signed by those who choose to be bound by them.
Subject to changeTimings are confirmed as faculty are finalised. Registered delegates are notified of any change by email and in the delegate app before it appears here. Allied health and trainee streams run in parallel; see workshops.
