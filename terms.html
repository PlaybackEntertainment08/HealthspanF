Delegate terms

Terms, cancellation & refunds

The conditions on which a registration is accepted. Last updated 11 October 2026.

1 · Who these terms are between

“The organisers” means the International Association of Stemcell & Regenerative Medicine (IASRM), which convenes the Healthspan Standard 2027 summit in New Delhi on 10 and 11 April 2027. “You” means the person named on the registration. Completing a registration means you accept these terms.

2 · Eligibility

Attendance is limited to healthcare professionals, researchers, postgraduate trainees, allied health professionals and industry delegates working in longevity or regenerative medicine. The organisers may decline or cancel a registration that does not meet this condition, refunding the fee in full.

Reduced rates — postgraduate trainee and allied health — require proof, which is verified before the rate is released. A registration obtained on a reduced rate without valid entitlement may be upgraded to the full rate or cancelled without refund.

3 · Fees, rates and taxes

  • The rate that applies is the rate in force on the date payment is received, not the date the profile was created or the pass selected.
  • A pass selected but unpaid is held for seven days, after which the selection lapses and the then-current rate applies.
  • Rupee fees are exclusive of GST, charged at 18%. A GST invoice is issued within 48 hours of payment and is available from your dashboard.
  • Invoices raised in US dollars are treated as an export of service and carry no Indian GST.
  • Bank charges on wire transfers are borne by the remitter. A shortfall must be made good before the registration is confirmed.

4 · Cancellation and refunds

Written notice received by the secretariatRefund
On or before 30 June 202650% of the fee paid, excluding GST
1 July 2026 – 31 January 202725% of the fee paid, excluding GST
After 31 January 2027No refund
  • Cancellation must be in writing to info@iasrmglobal.org, quoting the delegate reference. The date the email is received governs.
  • All refunds are processed after the summit has closed, to the original payment method.
  • GST, once remitted to the authorities, cannot be refunded by the organisers.
  • Workshop fees follow the same schedule. If a workshop does not reach its minimum number, its fee is refunded in full regardless of date.
  • Non-attendance without written cancellation is not refundable.

5 · Transfers

A registration may be transferred to a colleague of equivalent eligibility at no charge until 31 March 2027. Write to the secretariat with both names, the delegate reference and the new delegate’s details. Transfers after that date are at the organisers’ discretion. A reduced-rate registration may only be transferred to someone entitled to the same rate.

6 · Visa refusal

If an international delegate is refused a visa despite a Ministry approval letter issued in their name, the registration fee is refunded in full less bank charges, on production of the written refusal, irrespective of the dates in clause 4. This does not apply where the application was made late, was incomplete, or was made under the wrong visa category.

7 · Changes to the programme

The programme, faculty, timings, halls and venue are subject to change. The organisers will notify registered delegates of material changes by email. A change of speaker, session order, hall or venue within New Delhi is not a ground for refund.

8 · Cancellation or postponement by the organisers

If the summit is cancelled by the organisers, registration fees are refunded in full. If it is postponed, registrations are carried to the new dates, and a delegate who cannot attend may request a full refund within thirty days of the new dates being announced.

The organisers are not liable for travel, accommodation or other costs incurred by a delegate in either case, and are not liable for a failure to perform caused by an event beyond their reasonable control, including government restriction, public-health measures, civil disturbance or the unavailability of the venue.

9 · Conduct

Delegates are expected to engage with the arguments and not with the people making them. Challenging a claim from the floor is encouraged; the house rule exists for that purpose. Harassment, intimidation and abuse are not tolerated, and the organisers may remove a delegate without refund.

Recording, photographing or streaming any session is not permitted without written consent. Panel II, the Charter session, may not be recorded by anyone.

10 · The Experience Lounge

The Experience Lounge is an educational demonstration for professionals. It is not a medical assessment, no clinical relationship arises, and no result should be acted on without proper investigation. Participation is voluntary at every station.

11 · The Charter

Signing the Charter is voluntary and creates no legal obligation. Signatories are recorded by name and institution and published. A signatory may ask to be removed from the published list at any time by writing to the secretariat.

12 · The Longevity 40

Nomination is free at every stage. The panel’s decisions are final and are not subject to appeal or correspondence. No honour, shortlist place or sight of the shortlist is available for purchase, and any offer to the contrary is fraudulent.

13 · Images and recordings

The organisers photograph and film the summit for reporting and promotion. By attending you consent to appearing incidentally in such material. If you would rather not, tell the registration desk on arrival and you will be given a badge marker that the photography team respects.

14 · Liability

Delegates attend at their own risk and are strongly advised to hold travel and medical insurance. The organisers’ total liability to any delegate is limited to the registration fee paid. Nothing in these terms limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for fraud.

15 · Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of India, and the courts at New Delhi have exclusive jurisdiction.

Questions about these terms

Write to info@iasrmglobal.org. If something here is unclear before you register, ask first — we would rather explain it now than refund it later.
