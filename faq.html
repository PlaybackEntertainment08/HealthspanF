Before you write to us

Frequently asked questions

If the answer is not here, the secretariat replies within one working day.

Attending

Invited healthcare professionals — physicians, surgeons, researchers, allied health professionals, postgraduate trainees and industry delegates working in longevity and regenerative medicine. Registration asks for your medical council registration number or institutional affiliation. It is not open to the general public.

New Delhi, in the Aerocity district beside Indira Gandhi International Airport. The property is confirmed in December 2026 and registered delegates are emailed the address and floor plan before it appears on this site. See venue & Delhi.

English throughout, including the panels and the Charter session.

Plenary sessions are recorded and released to registered delegates for ninety days afterwards, where the speaker has consented. Panel II — the Charter session — is deliberately not recorded, so that delegates can argue without worrying about a clip.

Accreditation is sought from the Delhi Medical Council for the main programme and for each accredited workshop separately. Points are confirmed in February 2027 and printed on your attendance certificate. Enter your registration number in your profile and scan in at the hall door.

Business dress for the sessions; the halls run cool, so bring a layer. For the Gala Dinner on 10 April, black tie is optional and Indian formal is equally welcome.

Fees, invoicing and refunds

Both days of the scientific programme, the rapid-fire hall, the Experience Lounge, the delegate kit with all three launched books, both luncheons, refreshments, the Gala Dinner on 10 April, trade exhibition access and the attendance certificate. Residential passes add two nights with breakfast. Full inclusions.

Yes, at 18% on top of the listed rupee fee. Invoices raised in US dollars are treated as an export of service and carry no Indian GST. Add your GSTIN during registration if you need input credit; a GST invoice is issued within 48 hours of payment.

The rate in force on the date your payment is received, not the date you created your profile. Early closes 31 December 2026; standard closes 15 March 2027; after that the late and on-site rate applies.

Yes. Choose USD at registration and pay by international card, or ask for a SWIFT proforma invoice. Bank charges on wire transfers are borne by the remitter.

Transfers are free until 31 March 2027 — write to the secretariat with both names. Cancellations are refunded at 50% (to 30 June 2026), 25% (to 31 January 2027) or not at all thereafter, in each case excluding GST, and processed after the summit. Full policy.

Yes to both. Postgraduate trainees upload a letter from their head of department or a current student ID during registration; the rate is released once it is verified, normally within two working days. Allied health and nursing delegates register directly at that rate.

Institutions sending ten or more are invoiced directly, with a single GST invoice and one named coordinator. Ask for a group quotation.

Travel, access and everything else

Apply under the e-Business visa (e-B5V) category — the e-Conference visa no longer exists. Register and pay, then submit your passport details through the dashboard; we issue an invitation letter within five working days and apply for Ministry approval on your behalf. Start by 31 October 2026 if you can. Full guidance.

If a visa is refused despite a Ministry approval letter issued in your name, your fee is refunded in full less bank charges, on production of the written refusal.

Tell us in the access field of your profile. The venue is step-free with lift access to every hall, accessible toilets on the conference level, reserved seating and a hearing loop in Halls I and II. Requests made before 1 March 2027 can be arranged in full.

Yes, as an accompanying person — meals, the Gala Dinner, the exhibition and the spouse lounge, but not the scientific halls. Children under five are complimentary; older children register as accompanying persons. Delhi tours run while the halls are sitting.

Vegetarian, Jain, vegan, non-vegetarian and halal are all catered. Set your preference in your profile before 1 March 2027 and note any allergy in the same field.

No third-party housing agency is authorised to act for this summit, and the secretariat never asks for card details or transfers by message. Forward anything suspicious to info@iasrmglobal.org and do not pay.
