Your data

Privacy notice

What we collect, why, who sees it, and when it is deleted. Last updated 11 October 2026.

The short versionWe collect what we need to register you, invoice you, get you a visa and feed you correctly. We do not sell delegate data, and no sponsor receives your contact details. Measurements taken in the Experience Lounge belong to you and are deleted after ninety days.

1 · Who is responsible

The International Association of Stemcell & Regenerative Medicine (IASRM), New Delhi, is the data fiduciary for personal data processed in connection with the Healthspan Standard 2027 summit. Contact: info@iasrmglobal.org.

2 · What we collect, and why

3 · Payment data

Card details are collected and processed by the payment gateway on its own PCI-DSS-compliant pages. They are never transmitted to, seen by, or stored on this website or by IASRM. We receive only a transaction reference, the amount, the status and the last four digits of the instrument.

4 · Who we share data with

  • The payment gateway — to take payment and process refunds.
  • The Ministry of External Affairs and Ministry of Home Affairs — passport details, for visa approvals, and only for delegates who request a visa letter.
  • Partner hotels — name and stay dates only, for delegates who book a residential package.
  • The Delhi Medical Council — attendance and registration number, for delegates claiming CME credit.
  • Our email and hosting providers — under contract, to deliver the service.

We do not share delegate contact details with sponsors, exhibitors, Experience Lounge vendors or any third party for marketing. We do not sell data. An exhibitor only receives your details if you scan your own badge at their stand, and you may decline.

5 · The Experience Lounge

Measurements taken at the assessment stations are health data and are treated as such. They are stored under your delegate record, are available to you alone through your dashboard, and are deleted after ninety days. Where a station is operated by an industry partner, that partner may not retain your readings and may not contact you afterwards.

6 · Photography and recording

The summit is photographed and filmed. If you would rather not appear, tell the registration desk on arrival and you will be given a badge marker the photography team respects. Session recordings are released only where the speaker has consented; Panel II is not recorded at all.

7 · Cookies

This site sets no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. It uses browser storage for two purposes only: to keep you signed in to the delegate portal for the length of your session, and to remember the pass you were configuring so it survives a page reload. Analytics, where enabled, are aggregated and do not identify you.

8 · Your rights

You may ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it for a particular purpose. You may withdraw consent to marketing emails at any time, and every email we send carries an unsubscribe link. Some data must be kept despite a deletion request — invoices, for example, which Indian tax law requires us to retain.

Write to info@iasrmglobal.org. We respond within thirty days. If you are not satisfied with our response you may complain to the relevant data protection authority.

9 · Security

The site is served over TLS. Passwords are stored hashed and salted, never in plain text. Access to delegate records is limited to secretariat staff who need it. If a breach affecting your data occurs, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

10 · Changes

If this notice changes materially before the summit, registered delegates are emailed. The date at the top of this page always shows when it was last revised.
