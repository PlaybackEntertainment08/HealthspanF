Nominations are open

Nominate a colleague

Anyone may nominate, and it costs nothing. You do not have to be a delegate, and you may nominate as many people as you believe deserve it.

1 · Who you are nominating
Used only to confirm they exist and are willing to be considered.
If more than one fits, choose the strongest. The panel may move a nomination.
2 · Why
Up to 400 words. The panel reads this before anything else.
3 · Two referees

Both are contacted independently. A nomination with no reachable referee does not proceed to scoring.

4 · About you

Free, at every stage. Nominations close 31 January 2027, 23:59 IST.

Before you write

The panel reads several hundred of these. What distinguishes a strong nomination is not warmth but specificity.

  • Name the thing they did. A registry, a protocol, a trial, a clinic in a district town.
  • Say over what period. Sustained work scores above a single achievement.
  • Say what changed. For whom, and how you know.
  • Include the inconvenient part. A nominee who published their complication rate is stronger, not weaker, for it.
There is no entry feeNot for the nomination, not for the shortlist, not for the ceremony. Honourees attend the summit free and are not charged for the citation or the dinner.

What happens next

  1. Acknowledgement by email, immediately.
  2. Eligibility screen by the secretariat.
  3. Both referees contacted, February 2027.
  4. Independent scoring by the panel.
  5. Honourees told in confidence, March 2027.
  6. Citations read from the stage, 10 April 2027.
