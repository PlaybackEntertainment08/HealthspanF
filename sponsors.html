Industry sessions

Platinum and above may run a 30-minute industry session in a dedicated slot. It is labelled as an industry session in the programme, in the app and from the chair. The house rule applies: evidence tiers on every slide.

Experience Lounge stations

A limited number of assessment stations are available to diagnostics and device partners. Each station must display the evidence tier behind its primary claim, written by the scientific committee, not by the sponsor.

Gala Dinner & hospitality

Tables at the Gala Dinner, the welcome reception and the faculty dinner are available separately. Hospitality does not carry any programme rights.

What sponsorship does not buy

  • A place on the faculty, or the right to nominate one
  • Any influence on session titles, panel motions or chairs
  • Any Longevity 40 honour, shortlist place or sight of the shortlist
  • Exemption from the house rule on evidence tiers
  • Access to the delegate list, or to any delegate’s contact details
  • Editorial control over any part of the Charter

These rules are published because a sponsor who objects to them should find out now rather than in March.
