From the stage

Three books, unveiled

Each is launched in a slot of its own — a reading, then a short conversation with the authors, then questions.

Day One · 08:55

The Healthspan Standard

The book the summit is named for: a working manual for grading what you are about to tell a patient.

It takes the four-tier scheme used from the stage and applies it to the interventions a physician is actually asked about each week — the supplement, the test panel, the hormone, the clinic abroad. Each chapter ends with a page a clinician can hand across the desk.

Published to coincide with the summit and given to every delegate in the conference kit.

Included with every pass Launch: Sat 10 April, 08:55
THE HEALTHSPAN STANDARD
REGENERATIVE INTELLIGENCE

Day One · 14:00

Regenerative Intelligence

What regenerative medicine has actually shown in humans, and what it has only shown in a dish.

A reading of the trial literature on mesenchymal stromal cells, exosomes, platelet-rich plasma and orthobiologics — including the trials that failed, the registries that stopped reporting, and the gap between a mechanism and an outcome.

The final section is a consent framework for interventions supported by animal or mechanistic data alone.

Signing at the exhibition Launch: Sat 10 April, 14:00

Day Two · 08:40

The 10 PM Club

The measurable cost of late nights, written for the patient rather than for the clinician.

A short, plain book on sleep and circadian health that a physician can recommend without first having to correct it. It covers what consumer trackers measure and what they only estimate, shift work, the Indian evening, and the small number of changes that have outcome data behind them.

Launched on the Sunday morning, immediately before Session VII on sleep and circadian health.

Written for patients Launch: Sun 11 April, 08:40
