Scientific Committee

Who reviews the programme

Eleven reviewers — one for each session — plus two who read every submission for the house rule alone.

The scientific committee sets the session titles, invites the chairs, reviews every submitted abstract blind, and selects the thirty rapid-fire speakers. It also holds the only veto on the programme: a lecture whose slides do not carry evidence tiers does not go on.

Reviewers declare their interests before the review round opens, and recuse themselves from any abstract submitted by their own institution or by a company in which they hold an interest.

Abstract review

Blind, two reviewers per abstract, a third on disagreement.

Faculty invitation

By the committee, not by the sponsors.

Tier audit

Every slide deck, before the hall opens.

The separation rule

Three bodies, three mandates, no overlapping membership:

  1. Organising team — the event.
  2. Scientific committee — the programme.
  3. Longevity 40 jury — the honours.

No person sits on more than one. Sponsors sit on none of them.

The jury is independentThe Longevity 40 panel is appointed by IASRM and then works without reference to it. There is no entry fee, and no honour is purchasable. How the panel works.
